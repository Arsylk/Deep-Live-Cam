Place already-prepared local model assets in this folder.

The application never downloads models at startup. Legacy INSwapper expects
`inswapper_128.onnx` (or the optional local FP16 variant). Native-256 bundles
belong in `swappers/native_256/` and must include their hash-pinned
`manifest.json`, identity map, conditioner, and generator.
An ncnn-capable native-256 bundle additionally pins the conditioner and
generator `.ncnn.param`/`.ncnn.bin` pairs in that same manifest; create it only
with `tools/prepare_native256_ncnn.py`.
